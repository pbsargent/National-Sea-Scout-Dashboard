# National Sea Scout Dashboard project rules

Preserve Peter's inherited Scout storage, cleanup and membership-terminology instructions.

## Renewal rule confirmed November 6, 2026

Use current calendar month plus next calendar month, anchored to the dashboard
refresh date. September 2026 has two calendar months of grace through November
30; October 2026 has two calendar months of grace through December 31. No grace
continues after December 31, 2026; do not repeat this exception in later years.
December renewals become Past on January 1. Keep backend counts, national/CST
filters, board statuses, explanations and documentation aligned. Run pinned
before/on/after cutoff tests and preserve the guarded refresh source contract.
See docs/renewal-window-rule.md for the authoritative local definition.

## National youth headlines

Use From Excel AI2/AK2 for Total Youth/change and AI3/AK3 for Primary Youth/change.
AI3 includes Scouts without a unit from AI13. Read comparator date from AJ1 and baseline
values AJ2/AJ3 each refresh. Never replace national headline changes with sums
of ship-level YoY. These national youth cards stay national under ship filters.

## Scouts without a unit — confirmed November 6, 2026

Refer to the AI13 population as **Scouts without a unit**, never as Lone Scouts
in dashboard labels, summaries, or narrative. Peter clarified that this is
not the Lone Scout membership category. Preserve the count and its inclusion
in Primary Youth. The workbook's original AI13 label and the legacy
`loneScouts` JSON identifier may be retained only for provenance/compatibility;
they must not determine displayed terminology. Do not change source originals.
