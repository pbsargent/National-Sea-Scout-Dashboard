Start here

Quick Start

  1. Check the refresh date. This dashboard is a published workbook snapshot. Its date and capture name appear above.
  2. Start with National. Review the summary cards, then open a CST dashboard for a focused view.
  3. Narrow the view. Combine Search, Owner, Aux District, Focus, and one quick filter.
  4. Expand the health board. Open a CST, then a council, then select a ship to populate Ship Detail above the board.
  5. Confirm the record. Use the status explanations in Ship Detail and verify follow-up needs against the source workbook.

Controls

Filters, Search, and Detail

Search
Matches ship number, ship name, council, district, skipper, or charter organization. Search ignores letter case.
Owner / Aux District / Focus
Choose a source category. All removes that category’s restriction. These filters combine with search and the active quick filter.
All
Removes the quick-filter restriction only. Clear Search and return all three dropdowns to All to restore the full page view.
Growing
Includes ships with Total Youth YOY greater than zero. This differs from the workbook’s Growth health flag.
Training Gaps
Includes ships where either the Skipper or Committee Chair is not marked trained, including Partial and Gap.
Renewal Window
Includes ships whose parsed renewal date is from 0 through 120 days after the dashboard refresh timestamp.

Summary cards, board groups, and charts recalculate for matching ships. CST navigation counts describe the full captured dataset, rather than the active search. Filters apply to the current page; choose them again after navigating to another page.

Interpretation

Health Status and Color

How ship status labels are assigned
MeasureLabels and rules
Size, Growth, Advance, OutdoorHealthy (green) when the corresponding workbook flag is Yes; otherwise Needs Attention (red). Advance uses 20% Advance. These flags are supplied by the workbook, not recalculated from youth counts.
TrainingComplete (green): both roles trained. Partial (amber): exactly one. Gap (red): neither.
RenewalNext 120 (amber): within the forward 120-day window. Past (red): before refresh. Later (gray): beyond the window. Unscheduled (gray): no usable renewal date.
MetricStrong (green): 4 or higher. Watch (amber): 2–3. Risk (red): below 2.

Renewal timing: the source month (such as Jul-26) is parsed as the first day of that month and compared with the refresh timestamp, not today’s date. Past is a timing cue; it does not independently confirm a ship’s registration has lapsed.

Green = healthy or complete; amber = watch or approaching action; red = attention needed; gray = informational. Read the text label as well as its color. The meanings stay the same in System, Light, and Dark themes.

Navigation

Choose the Right View

National dashboard →All captured ships, national membership totals, CST → council → ship drill-down, and supporting charts.CST dashboards →Choose a CST in the dashboard navigation. Each page starts with that CST’s ships and drills from council to ship. Its Councils card counts distinct councils in the filtered view.

Use Expand all or Collapse all to open or close board groups. Select a ship row to inspect its membership, leadership, renewal, and status explanations. Use its BeAScout link on the row or in Ship Detail to open the verified unit information page in a new tab. If a destination could not be verified, Ship Detail says so.

Reference

Read the Summary Cards

Total Ships / Ships
Number of included source rows with a UnitID. The note counts rows whose Status is New.
Total Youth
Sum of Total Youth for the current view. The YOY note sums the source Total Youth YOY changes; it is a member-count change, not a percentage.
Primary Youth
National view: sum of Primary Youth, with the summed Primary YOY change beneath it. These are the workbook’s primary-registration counts.
Councils
CST view: number of distinct councils represented by the matching ships.
Training Coverage / Training
Two separate percentages: Skipper first, Committee Chair second. Each divides ships marked trained for that role by all ships in the current view, rounded to a whole percent. It is not the percentage with both roles trained.
Avg Unit Metric / Avg Metric
Mean of the source Unit Metric values across ships in view, shown to two decimal places on the workbook’s 0–5 scale.

Adult totals appear in Ship Detail. Membership is summed from ship records; the dashboard does not deduplicate individual people across ships.

Health board

What Progress Means

Progress is the percentage of six positive checks: Size healthy, Growth healthy, Advance healthy, Outdoor healthy, both leadership roles trained, and Unit Metric at least 3.

For a ship, five checks out of six gives 83%. For a council or CST, the board divides all positive checks by six times the number of ships, then rounds to a whole percent. Renewal is not part of Progress.

A metric of 3 contributes to Progress while still showing a Watch metric label. Progress is a dashboard summary of these checks, not the workbook’s Unit Metric score.

Colored group bars show the share of ships in each status. Hover over a group bar for its status counts; expand the group to review individual ships.

Explore

Read the Supporting Charts

National / CST Health Mix
Ship counts grouped into Unit Metric buckets: 0–1, 2, 3, and 4–5.
Ship Focus / Aux Districts
Ship counts grouped by Ship Focus or Coast Guard Auxiliary district. Each chart shows up to eight categories, ranked by count.
Largest Youth Councils / Councils by Youth
Up to ten councils ranked by summed Total Youth in the current view.
Renewal Load
Ship counts grouped by the source renewal label, showing up to ten categories ranked by count. This is a workload breakdown, not a chronological timeline or a list limited to upcoming renewals.

Bar lengths are relative to the largest displayed value in each chart. Charts may show fewer categories than exist in the full dataset.

Monthly Trends

Open Monthly Trends for national youth, adult registrations, ships, and youth per ship from the TY History worksheet. Summary cards show the latest recorded month and its change from the same month one year earlier.

Choose a measure and year to update both the graph and table. The table is grouped into expandable year sections, with the latest year open initially. Select a year heading to expand or collapse its months. Timeline shows observations in date order. Year-over-year comparison aligns each year from January through December. Focus or hover over graph points for dates and values. Download CSV exports the selected measure and year with exact values and comparison columns.

Monthly change compares the preceding calendar month. Yearly change compares the same calendar month in the previous year. Percentage change divides the change by that earlier value. A missing or zero baseline makes the percentage unavailable. Youth per ship divides total youth by ships.

Missing months are gaps in the graph and Unavailable in the table; no values are interpolated. Observation dates are preserved, including February 28, 2024. Monthly totals can differ from annual history and the current Ships capture. These are separate source series. The page has national totals only and does not establish unique-person counts or council and ship histories.

Sources and limitations

Where the Data Comes From

The dashboard uses the Ships worksheet from the newest dated SeaScoutShips-YYYY-MM-DD.xlsx capture in NSSC Membership Spreadsheet Capture. Only rows with a UnitID are included. The dashboard does not query live membership or training records while you browse.

Dashboard fields and workbook columns
Dashboard fieldsWorkbook columns
Identity and geographyShip #, UnitID, Ship Name if tracking, CST, Council Name, District
Assignments and programOwner, CG Aux Districts, Ship Focus, Status
Membership and changeTotal Youth, Primary Youth, Total Adult, Total Youth YOY, Primary YOY
Health and trainingSize, Growth, 20% Advance, Outdoor, Skipper Trained, CC Trained, Unit Metric
Renewal and leadershipRenewal, Charter Organization, Skipper Name

Missing values matter. Blank Owner and Aux District become Unassigned; blank Focus becomes Unspecified; blank Status becomes Established. Missing or unparseable numeric counts and metrics become zero. Health and training flags not recognized as Yes, True, or 1 become false. A red status or zero can therefore reflect incomplete source data.

The public CSV snapshot contains approved ship-level fields. Emails, phone numbers, contact names, and internal notes are excluded. Skipper names use first name plus last initial in the CSV and dashboard. The dashboard data file records the capture name and refresh timestamp. Verify important discrepancies in the source workbook before acting.

Support

Troubleshooting

A ship or council is missing
Clear Search, set Owner, Aux District, and Focus to All, and choose the All quick filter. Check whether you are on the correct CST page. Expand the board groups. If the ship is still absent, verify that its source row has a UnitID.
The totals differ from another report
Compare capture dates, active filters, source definitions, and ship-level versus unique-person counts. YOY comes from the workbook’s supplied comparison fields.
A status looks wrong
Select the ship and read its status explanations. Check the source Yes/No fields, Unit Metric, and renewal month. Missing flags can appear as Needs Attention.
The board is cut off on a phone
Scroll horizontally within the health board to reach the remaining status columns. Summary cards and Ship Detail appear above the board.
The data seems stale or the page is empty
Reload the page and check the refresh date. Clear filters. If the issue persists, report the page, CST, refresh date, filters, ship UnitID, and expected result.

Email dashboard support. Include only the information needed to investigate; avoid sharing unnecessary personal details.
